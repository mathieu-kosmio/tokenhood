# Pilot campaign and continuation criteria

The pilot campaign is used to decide what comes next. It starts in lot 0 on the Tokenhood project and ends in lot 3 with at least one external project; its detailed protocol is produced by mission TH-010.

**Exploratory sizing**, with no claim to statistical representativeness:

- 15 to 20 real missions, of varied types (code, documentation, i18n, governance);
- several contributors, including at least one from outside the founding team;
- two different agent clients when available;
- at least one external maintainer, on a manually admitted project.

**What is recorded**: all missions, including abandoned ones; blockages and their cause; human interventions (who, when, for how long); resumptions; changes after review; defects after integration; 30-day feedback; gaps between estimate and consumption.

**Three demonstrations condition continuation**

1. **The loop works reproducibly**: success does not depend on a discreet and permanent intervention by the founders.
2. **The beneficiary project really gains from it**: the time and difficulties transferred to the maintainer remain acceptable, in their view.
3. **The limits are known and respected**: non-compatible clients, estimated measurements and non-verifiable protections are presented as such.

No numerical threshold is set in advance. Continuing, adjusting or stopping is a collective governance decision, taken on the basis of the campaign report (mission TH-306).
